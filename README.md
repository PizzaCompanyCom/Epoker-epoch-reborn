# EpokerWoW Raid — Poker in World of Warcraft!

**Version 1.8.0 RC1 | WoW 3.3.5a | TrinityCore**

**EpokerWoW Raid** is a multiplayer poker addon that lets you play poker with your friends directly inside World of Warcraft!

## Two Poker Games

**♠ Ironforge 5-Card Draw**
Classic five-card poker. Place bets, exchange cards, bluff your opponents, and build the strongest hand!

**♥ The Barrens Hold'em**
Texas Hold'em with two private cards, five community cards, multiple betting rounds, and All-In action.

## Main Features

- Multiplayer poker within a raid group.
- Game Master system with transferable table control.
- Virtual Gold & Silver balances — no automatic gold transfers.
- Player Management with seating, balances, and player statuses.
- No-Limit betting, All-In, and poker hand rankings.
- Turn Timer with automatic Check or Fold.
- Player statistics and game history.
- Custom poker tables and Warcraft-themed cards.
- Emotes: Cry, Cheer, Roar, Train, and Chicken.
- Chat announcements for betting actions and winners.
- Reconnect and synchronization tools.

## How to Play

1. Install the addon and join a raid group with other players.
2. Open **EpokerWoW Raid** and select your poker game.
3. Create or join a poker table.
4. The table creator becomes **Game Master**.
5. The Game Master assigns player balances and manages seats.
6. Start a hand, place your bets, and play your cards!
7. Win the pot with the strongest poker hand — or make everyone fold!

## Important Information

All players should install the same addon version for multiplayer compatibility.

**Gold and Silver are virtual poker balances.** The addon does not automatically transfer real in-game currency.If you are playing for gold or silver, you must send your stake to the game master, who will then record that amount in your account within the add-on. Once the game concludes, the game master distributes the balances to the players.

**Main Menu**

Ironforge 5-Card Draw — Play classic five-card poker.

The Barrens Hold'em — Play Texas Hold'em.

**Game Controls**

Tables — Create or join a poker table.

Join / Leave Table — Enter or leave the game.

Start Hand — Begin a new poker round.

Poker Table — Open the visual poker table.

**Player Management — Manage players, balances, seats, and turn timer.**

Betting Activity — View recent betting actions.

Statistics — View player statistics.

History — Review previous hands.

Settings — Configure the addon.

**Help / Rankings — Read the rules and poker hand rankings.**

Betting Buttons

Check — Pass without betting when no call is required.

Call — Match the current bet.

Bet — Place a bet.

Raise — Increase the current bet.

Fold — Give up your hand.

All-In — Bet your entire remaining balance.

Exchange (5-Card Draw) — Replace selected cards.

Stand Pat (5-Card Draw) — Keep all five cards.

**Game Master Controls**

Sit In / Sit Out — Include or exclude a player from upcoming hands.

Set Balance — Change a player's virtual Gold/Silver.

Force Fold — Fold a player's hand.

Transfer GM — Assign a new Game Master.

Turn Timer — Set the time allowed for each turn.

Force Synchronization — Refresh shared table information.

Reconnect / Recovery — Restore the available table session state.

**Emotes & Announcements**

Emotes — Open the emotes and announcements window.

Cry / Cheer / Roar / Train / Chicken — Perform character emotes.

Announce Winner — Share the last hand's result in chat.

Auto Announce — Automatically announce betting actions.

Chat Channel — Choose where announcements are sent.
