Raid 5-Card Poker WotLK v1.6.4 — Interface & Sound Update
WoW 3.3.5a / TrinityCore; English UI.

- Top-right toolbar: same-size 30x30 WoW-style Settings, Rankings, Rules, Help icons; Solo Debug remains.
- Rankings keeps its own poker-hand ranking reference window.
- Rules opens a separate Five-Card Draw rules window.
- Help opens a separate GM and Player onboarding window for multi-table play.
- /poker rules and /poker help open their respective windows.
- Stand Pat remains a separate action.
- Sounds: bet coins, your turn, card exchange, new hand, showdown.
- Settings > Sound Options: master toggle and individual event toggles.
- Sound effects use built-in WoW client files; no server changes.
- Sound settings persist in SavedVariables.
- Multiple independent tables, Table Browser, Player Management and Busted preserved.

NOTE: Sound path availability varies between WoW client distributions; verify sounds in your 3.3.5a client. Sound playback is local-only.
INSTALL: Replace Interface/AddOns/RaidPoker_WotLK, then /reload. All players should use v1.6.4.
